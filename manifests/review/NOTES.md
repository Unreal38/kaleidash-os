# Inventory review notes

This first split intentionally favors a small reproducible desktop over copying historical state.

## Not replayed

- The 358 `Group`-reason RPM records belong to the Fedora KDE substrate.
- The 124 `External User` records are mostly dependencies or packages introduced by external RPM transactions. Only known top-level applications are represented in `external.txt`.
- Kernel, kernel module, firmware, installer, live-image, rescue, and architecture-specific boot packages are resolved by Fedora on the target machine.
- Versioned Snap mount units are generated by Snap and must never be enabled directly.
- Default system and user services are left to Fedora rather than copied wholesale.
- SDDM packages remain part of the KDE substrate, but KaleidashOS activates greetd and Noctalia Greeter.

## Explicitly machine-specific

- `actions.runner.<redacted>.service` represents a workstation runner, not part of KaleidashOS.
- `qemu-guest-agent`, `vboxservice`, `vgauthd`, `vmtoolsd`, and Hyper-V guest services are not portable bare-metal defaults.
- `joycond.service` needs a controller-profile package/source decision before automation.
- `ecbd.service` needs its owning package and purpose identified before automation.

## Repositories not carried forward

- `copr:copr.fedorainfracloud.org:atim:starship` is redundant because the captured Starship package came from Terra.
- `copr:copr.fedorainfracloud.org:g3tchoo:prismlauncher` is redundant because the captured Prism Launcher package came from Terra.
- `copr:copr.fedorainfracloud.org:codifryed:CoolerControl` is stale and conflicts with the chosen direct `liquidctl` approach.

## Pending source recipes

- Consulo, SourceGit, Unity Hub, PureRef, and Epson Printer Utility need stable vendor-source recipes.
- `io.github.ishidawg.LeShade` references `leshade-origin`, but that Flatpak remote was not present in the captured remote list. It is withheld until its source is defined.

## Later configuration capture

Package manifests do not yet reproduce Niri bindings, Noctalia layout/plugins, Kitty/Fish/Starship configuration, Yazi, GTK/Qt/KDE styling, Firefox theming, audio channel swapping, Sunshine display scripts, or liquidctl policy. Those belong to the next configuration-capture milestone and must exclude credentials and device secrets.
